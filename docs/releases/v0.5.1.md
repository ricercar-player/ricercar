**ricercar 0.5.1** smooths the everyday: one click plays, the player bar leads to albums and artists, and search puts your own music first.

## Downloads

| Your system | File | Install |
|---|---|---|
| Debian 12+, Ubuntu 22.04+, Mint, Pop!_OS, Zorin, elementary | `ricercar_0.5.1-1_amd64.deb` / `_arm64.deb` | `sudo apt install ./ricercar_*.deb` |

Verify downloads with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS --ignore-missing`.


## What's new

- **One click plays.** A single click on a track, a queue row or a radio station starts it; buttons in a row keep their own action.
- **The player bar leads somewhere.** Its title opens the album and its artist opens the artist page, for plugin tracks too.
- **Artist pages for any artist.** An artist page now also lists the artist's albums from your plugins' catalogues, not only from your favourites there.
- **Your library first in search.** Results start with your own music: local files and playlists, then the playlists and favourites your plugins hold for you, then the catalogues. A switch next to the kind filter searches **My library** only.
- **Fixes:**
  - notifications show the cover of plugin tracks;
  - the signal path names PipeWire and Bluetooth outputs by their name, and long device names no longer spill out of the player bar;
  - in the expanded now-playing view, the Queue button opens its Queue tab.
  - in a narrow window, the descriptions of the plugin catalogue no longer overlap.

> Plugins are written by third parties and are not reviewed by the ricercar project. They run with your permissions: install only plugins you trust, and check that they respect the terms of the service they use.
